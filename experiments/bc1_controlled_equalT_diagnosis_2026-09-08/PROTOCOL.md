# Frozen protocol: projection-free BC1 equal-temperature profile diagnostic

Frozen before any production output exists. This is a targeted known-answer
diagnostic, not a boundary-condition sweep.

## Scientific question

At equal temperatures, the BC1 canonical bath has invariant density
`rho proportional to exp(-H/(2T))`. Because `H` depends on phases only through
bond cosines, phase reversal changes every `sin(theta_j)` sign without changing
the density. Therefore every stationary `mean(sin(theta_j))` is exactly zero.

The inherited SIMD profile study violated this identity systematically. This
experiment asks whether that violation persists under the already validated
projection-free Cartesian integrator.

## Controlled dynamics and fixed integrator

The source is ported from
`experiments/spectral_gap_controlled_2026-09-07/source/NLS_stationary_autocorr_controlled.cpp`
(SHA-256 `58c871882f1180701a7aae637a8f4be113315a72fb9d7815562f5fc90faf7976`).
It preserves the same update and RNG ordering while generalizing the chain
length and adding profile accumulators:

```text
c_j = x_j + i y_j, I_j = |c_j|^2, E = H/2,
dc_j = i grad_j(E) dt                                 (interior),
dc_r = (i-gamma) grad_r(E) dt + sqrt(2 gamma T_r) dW  (r=1,n).
```

Each fixed step is an implicit-midpoint Hamiltonian update followed by the two
Euler--Maruyama Cartesian bath updates, with left/right bath order alternating
by step. State and arithmetic are double precision; standard-library
trigonometry is used. There is no action floor, positive-action projection,
adaptive step, or polynomial trigonometric approximation.

Profiles are sampled every `0.01` time units after burn-in, including both
measurement endpoints. Each reported uncertainty is the standard error across
independent trajectory-level stationary means; time samples are never treated
as independent replicates.

## Frozen production matrix

- BC1 only; `T1=Tn=6`, `gamma=0.1`, `dt=5e-4`.
- `n=25`: burn-in `2000`, measurement duration `2000`.
- `n=100`: burn-in `32000`, measurement duration `2000`.
- Two independent base seeds per chain length:
  - `n=25`: `2026090825`, `2026090826`;
  - `n=100`: `2026090900`, `2026090901`.
- Per seed: 16 deterministic SIMD batches x 16 lanes = 256 trajectories.
- Merged per chain length: 512 independent trajectories.
- Batch stream offsets are `0,16,...,240`; stream seeds are generated by the
  unchanged `stream_seed(base_seed, stream_offset+lane)` function.
- At most eight single-batch processes run concurrently and only on AC power.
- A completed batch is never overwritten; interrupted partial output is kept.

The fixed trajectory count and measurement duration match the SIMD study and
are intended to give comparable bondwise standard errors. No adaptive sample
extension is permitted after viewing the z-scores.

## Frozen sequential porting gate

The two `n=25` seeds are completed and analyzed before any `n=100` production.
The port passes at a chain length only if both conditions hold:

1. the controlled spatial mean action differs from the SIMD reference by at
   most 5%;
2. the controlled action-profile relative range `(max-min)/mean` is no larger
   than the corresponding SIMD relative range.

Frozen SIMD action references:

| n | spatial mean | relative range |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 0.494670165647086 | 0.00893622658 |
| 100 | 0.249219499939971 | 0.0973137457 |

If `n=25` fails, the pipeline stops before `n=100`; the sine comparison is
declared inadmissible. If `n=100` fails, its sine result is likewise marked
inadmissible.

## Frozen sine tests

For each bond,

```text
z_j = mean(sin(theta_j)) / SE_j.
```

- pointwise 95% failure: `abs(z_j) > 1.9599639845`;
- simultaneous 95% Bonferroni critical value:
  `Phi^-1(1 - 0.05/(2(n-1)))`;
- the known-answer control passes at a chain length only if the maximum
  absolute z-score does not exceed the Bonferroni critical value.

All bonds are retained. The report gives every mean, SE, z-score, the number of
pointwise failures, and the simultaneous result. It also gives controlled/SIMD
SE ratios without using them as a post-hoc stopping rule.

Frozen SIMD sine references:

| n | max abs(z) | pointwise failures | bondwise SE range |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 8.6781262893 | 19/24 | 0.0002285--0.0004949 |
| 100 | 12.9285247740 | 71/99 | 0.0001953--0.0007773 |

## Numerical-event audit

Report projection and floor counts (identically zero by construction),
zero-radius events, minimum sampled action, midpoint nonconvergence events, and
non-finite events. A midpoint or non-finite event is a numerical failure and is
preserved rather than hidden or retried with altered parameters.

## Interpretation gate

- If the porting gate and Bonferroni sine test pass, the inherited SIMD
  equal-temperature deviation is classified as a numerical artifact of that
  scheme. The driven `(10,2)` sine profiles must then be re-measured with the
  controlled integrator before quantitative use.
- If the controlled result still fails at comparable z-score, the projection
  hypothesis is not supported. Report residual numerical/dynamical candidates
  and whether the maximum z-score grows from `n=25` to `n=100`.
- No timestep, burn-in, sample count, sampling interval, seed, gate, or
  interpretation rule may be changed after production begins.
