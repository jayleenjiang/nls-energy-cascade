# Final verdict

Production is complete and internally auditable: all 72 frozen replicate runs finished, all required files exist, the BC1 reproduction gate passed, and no non-finite or discarded trajectory occurred.

The primary mechanism test is supported over n = 25, 50, 100. BC1/BC2, which retain the global M term, give midpoint-action exponents from -0.485 to -0.464; BC3/BC3b, which remove it, give exponents from +0.032 to +0.083. This is consistent with the proposed n^-1/2 versus n^0 contrast.

The complete experiment is not an all-gates PASS: the requested equal-temperature zero-sine control fails for every boundary condition. This limitation is explicit in the report. The results may support a carefully scoped numerical mechanism claim, but they do not justify claiming exact equilibrium profiles for the inherited SIMD discretization.
