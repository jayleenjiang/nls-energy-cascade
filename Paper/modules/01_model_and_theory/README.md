# Module 01: model, thermodynamics, and cascade geometry

## Manuscript coverage

Introduction; derivation of the model; known results; nonequilibrium
thermodynamics; Fokker--Planck and generator definitions.

## Scientific role

The analytical part defines the stochastic NLS cascade chain, its Hamiltonian
normalization, Gibbs-preserving endpoint baths, conserved action current, and
short-chain generator.  The only numerical object in this module is the CKSTT
frequency embedding used to connect abstract chain generations to resonant
Fourier-mode families.

## Geometry experiment

- Purpose: verify and visualize the first five resonant-family transitions.
- Generator: Energy Cascade/Rect Plots/main.cpp.
- Verification and plotting: verify_rectangles.py and plot_rectangles.py.
- Raw results: four rectangles_N5_R100_*.csv files.
- Manuscript output: Paper/revision/cascade_embedding.pdf.

## Status

The model and geometry framing are source-traced and compatible with the
advisor-synced scientific baseline.  They do not support any transport exponent
or open-chain terminal-energy claim.
