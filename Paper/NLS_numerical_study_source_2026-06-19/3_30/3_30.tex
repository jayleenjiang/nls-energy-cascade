We use noise-compensating KDE to estimate the steady-state density: each MC sample in a box contributes Gaussian weight $K_\sigma(x - x_c)$ centered at the box center, with $\sigma = H_{\mathrm{box}}/3$.

$T^*$ correction:
\[
T^* = T_{\mathrm{target}} - h^2 \langle I_1 I_2 \rangle < T_{\mathrm{target}}
\]

Experiment:
On 2D conditional slice ($\theta_1, \theta_3$) with $I \approx 2$, $T_{\mathrm{target}} = 5$, $30 \times 30$ grid, we sweep $T_{\mathrm{run}}$ and compare both NC-KDE ($w = K_\sigma$) and plain histogram ($w = 1$) against the fixed theoretical density at $T_{\mathrm{target}}$

\bigskip

standard KDE:
\[
\hat{f}(x_c) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} K_h(x_c - X_i)
\]
with $E[\hat{f}(x_c)] = (f * K_h)(x_c)$

\bigskip

NC-KDE (each sample contributes only to its own bin)
\[
\hat{f}_j = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{X_i \in \mathrm{bin}\, j} K_\sigma(x_j - X_i)
\]

For comparison, we test a standard KDE by sweeping $T_{\mathrm{run}}$ with KDE at diff $h$:

The RMSE minimum shifts left with increasing $h$, consistent with $T^* = T - h^2\langle I_1 I_2 \rangle$
