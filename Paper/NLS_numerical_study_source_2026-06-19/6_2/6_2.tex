\paragraph{Q1. Is the inner loop vectorized? 8/16 entries per cycle?}
Two implementations were tested.

\emph{Eigen version.} Compiled with
\texttt{-O3 -mcpu=native -ffast-math}, which lets the compiler emit
NEON/AVX instructions on the Eigen array operations over the $n=25$ modes.
Two possible issues limit this? 1. the time-stepping loop allocates several temporary
length-$n$ vectors at every step 2.$n=25$ is short relative to a SIMD
register. not full $8/16$ lanes per cycle. Runtime: $\sim 2.5$ hours with
$5\times 10^8$ samples

\emph{SIMD version.} structure-of-arrays
layout \texttt{I[mode*W + lane]} and \texttt{\#pragma omp simd} explicitly
vectorizing across $W=16$ trajectory lanes,
put 16 entries into one SIMD instruction (by trajectory, not by mode).
Measured speedup: $1.01\times 10^9$ samples in $25$ minutes

\paragraph{Q2. Fast sin / fast RNG?}
\emph{RNG.} Both versions: \texttt{std::mt19937} +
\texttt{std::normal\_distribution} replaced by a xoshiro256++ uniform
generator and a Box--Muller Gaussian with one cached value.

\emph{sin/cos.} Eigen version still calls \texttt{std::sin} and
\texttt{std::cos} (with \texttt{-ffast-math} the compiler may fuse
adjacent calls into \texttt{sincos}, but not a vectorized polynomial
approximation). SIMD version uses a Bhaskara-style polynomial
\texttt{fast\_sin}/\texttt{fast\_cos} (${\sim}10^{-3}$ accuracy) and
\texttt{fast\_log} for the Box--Muller step.

The Eigen version produces $5.07\times 10^8$ samples per site pair

\section*{Setup}
\begin{itemize}
\item Chain length $n=25$, Case~0 (closed chain), $\gamma=0.1$, $dt=0.001$.
\item Two conditions: NESS ($T_1=10$, $T_n=2$) and equilibrium ($T_1=T_n=6$).
\item Eigen runs: $N_{\mathrm{traj}}=256$, $T_{\mathrm{burnin}}=2000$,
$T_{\mathrm{final}}=200000$, sampling every $0.1$ time units;
$5.07\times 10^8$ samples per site pair.
\item SIMD runs: $32$ groups of $W=16$ lanes (512 chains),
$T_{\mathrm{final}}=200000$, sampling every $0.1$ time units;
$1.01\times 10^9$ samples per site pair.
\item Three site pairs in the bulk: $j=6, 12, 18$.
\item $80$ bins per axis on $[0,4]\times[0,4]\times[-\pi,\pi)$; overflow $<1\%$.
\item Statistical errors via Poisson resampling of the bin counts
($200$ bootstrap rounds).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Fit forms}
We fit $\log\rho(I_a,I_b,\theta)$ for an adjacent pair $(I_a,I_b)=(I_j,I_{j+1})$,
$\theta=\theta_j$, to three forms:
\begin{align*}
\text{Form A (conjecture)}\quad
& \log\rho = a + b\bigl[-\tfrac{1}{4}(I_a^2+I_b^2) + I_a I_b\cos\theta\bigr], \\
\text{Form B (strict local Gibbs)}\quad
& \log\rho = a + b\bigl[\tfrac{1}{4}(I_a^2+I_b^2) + I_a I_b(1+\cos\theta)\bigr], \\
\text{Form C (general quadratic)}\quad
& \log\rho = c_0 + c_1 I_a^2 + c_2 I_b^2 + c_3 I_a I_b + c_4 I_a I_b\cos\theta .
\end{align*}
Form B is obtained by restricting the Hamiltonian (summary eq.~2.6) to the terms
in $(I_{12}, I_{13}, \theta_{12})$. Form C lets the data choose all four
coefficients; strict local Gibbs (Form B) requires $c_1=c_2$ and $c_3=c_4$, with
$c_1/c_3 = 1/4$.

\section*{Result -- double-precision (Eigen) version}
\paragraph{Form A (conjecture) does not fit:} $R^2 \in [0.27, 0.33]$ across
all sites and both conditions.

\paragraph{At equilibrium:} All three sites give $c_3/c_4$ in the range
$1.47$--$1.62$ and $c_1/c_3 \approx 0.33$--$0.36$, with a bootstrap standard
deviation of ${\sim}0.0005$ on these ratios. The departures from $c_3/c_4=1$ and
$c_1/c_3=0.25$ are hundreds of $\sigma$.

\paragraph{At NESS:} The same ratios are smaller and depend on position.

The symmetry between $I_j$ and $I_{j+1}$ holds throughout:
$c_1/c_2 \in [0.99, 1.00]$ everywhere.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Under NESS, $c_3/c_4$ decreases monotonically from the hot to the cold
side ($1.22 \to 1.07 \to 1.03$), as does $c_1/c_3$ ($0.44 \to 0.35 \to 0.29$).
At equilibrium the three sites are flat to within ${\sim}6\%$, with no
monotonic trend.
\item The NESS range $c_3/c_4\in[1.03,1.22]$ does not overlap the equilibrium
range $[1.47,1.62]$.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Result -- SIMD (float, $10^9$ samples) version}
The SIMD runs were repeated at both conditions. At NESS, all three
qualitative findings are reproduced: Form A still fails
($R^2 \in [0.20, 0.25]$), strict local Gibbs is still rejected
($c_3/c_4 \neq 1$ and $c_1/c_3 \neq 0.25$ at all sites), and the
monotonic spatial gradient is reproduced with the same sign and
ordering. The thermal profile $\langle I_j\rangle$ matches the Eigen
run to $\sim 0.1\%$ at all $25$ modes.
